# Restrained Ronke theme pass

Reference research: https://ronkeverse.com/ and its official Ronke/wordmark
image links; https://linktr.ee/RonkeonRon; indexed public posts mentioning
@RonkeOnRon and $RONKE. Ronkeverse.fun and the live X timeline could not be
read directly with the available tools. The Blue Monke identity is confirmed by
the accessible official site; this palette is an interpretation, not a claim
of exact official brand hex values.

Palette: deep navy #101b3d, panel blue #192d56, line blue #3b5b87,
cyan #55c9f5, banana yellow #f6d35c, warm cream #fff1d6 and muted
blue #a9bfdc. Buttons, menus, selection outlines, scrollbars, touch-control
accents and banners use this palette. Arena textures get a restrained cool tint
and blue wash while keeping Verdant, Ember and Astral visually distinct.

An original transparent banana/mango/leaf flourish appears at small size on the
home card and camp screen, plus one faint stamp in the arena's upper-left trim.
It is decoration only. No fruit pickups, banana attacks, fruit currency or new
mechanics are added. The gorillas, enemy art, skill colours, pink hostile lanes,
warning labels and collision/gameplay rules are unchanged. Existing arena image
files are preserved; the cool treatment is applied while drawing.

The browser prototype is unchanged. Validation covers native desktop/mobile
UI/layout/drawing and packed resource loading on installed Godot 4.6.3. A real
graphical playtest on the user's 4.7.2 setup and Android remains outstanding.
